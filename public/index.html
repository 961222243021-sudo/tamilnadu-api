Know what you’re comparing

These are general-election cycle results, not current officeholders or a full by-election history. Constituency numbers before 2011 must not be equated with modern boundaries. Party labels follow the source for each year. Candidate search returns records and does not establish that similarly named people are the same person.

Some source totals conflict. Those records retain their votes but omit computed percentages. Tamil search, district filters, local-body results, and parliamentary elections are planned additions.
